CORBEL & FINCH HOME – SAFETY RECALL CF-R-0311 – CUSTOMER FAQ (issued 2026-03-11)

Which products are affected?
- Halo 900 oil-filled heater (SKU CF-H900-W, CF-H900-G)
- Halo 900 Slim oil-filled heater (SKU CF-H900S-W)
This covers production batches B0421 to B0433 and all units dispatched from 2026-02-20 to 2026-03-11. The batch code is printed on the side label of the outer carton.
- The Halo 700 and Halo 1200 are NOT affected.

What is the risk?
The thermostat may fail and the heater can overheat when it is switched on. A sealed, unplugged unit is not a hazard.

I already have one.
Stop using it, unplug it and email recalls@corbelfinch.example with your order number. We will book a free collection and give you a full refund once it has been collected.

My order is still on its way.
We have asked our carriers to return in-transit parcels to us. If a courier still tries to deliver, you can choose either option. Both get you a full refund.
  (a) Refuse delivery. The refund is issued automatically when the return scan reaches our warehouse, usually within 5 working days.
  (b) Accept it, keep it SEALED and UNPLUGGED, and email recalls@corbelfinch.example to book a free collection. The refund is issued after collection.
Do not open, test or use the unit.
