- **Stage:** Context
- **Channel:** Email
- **From:** Notification support, Plover Arc Logistics
- **To:** Partner desks (incl. Iona Quill, Rivet Delivery Desk)

Partner bulletin NS-0316: We are seeing a text-message phishing ('smishing') campaign that impersonates Plover Arc. Reminder of our standing policy: Plover Arc does not charge any fee for domestic redelivery and never asks for payment through an SMS link. Genuine texts come from sender ID 'PloverArc' and only link to ploverarc.example. Import duties are billed separately by Import billing and quote an INV- reference. If a customer reports a suspicious message, do not open the link. Send the message text, sender number and shipment reference to Notification support by email, and advise the customer not to enter payment details. Customers who have entered card details should contact their card issuer.
