Partner reminder HPP-0511 (pickup-point holds). A standard hold at a Hollin point lasts 5 days. It ends at 18:00 UTC on the expiry date shown in the collection notice. A partner desk may request one extension of up to 3 days by emailing Pickup-point operations before the hold ends. We confirm every extension by email. When a hold ends, the parcel is scanned out and collected on the 18:15 UTC return sweep, which takes it back to the carrier's depot. After a hold has ended, counter staff cannot extend it, re-shelve the parcel or release it, and we cannot reverse a sweep. For parcels that have been swept, please contact the carrier.

Partner bulletin SCH-0511: parcels returned from pickup points. Parcels swept from a Hollin point after their hold ends arrive at Brenmoor depot the same evening. They leave on the 07:00 UTC trunk the next morning for return to sender, unless Scheduling receives an instruction from the partner desk by 21:00 UTC on the day of the sweep. The partner desk can request one of these options, once per parcel and at no charge: (a) a re-route to a Hollin point of the recipient's choice, with a new 5-day hold that starts when the parcel is ready to collect (12:00 UTC the next day at the earliest); or (b) a home redelivery on a chosen day, Monday to Saturday.
