Partner bulletin INV-B-0511 explains how to raise a trace for a parcel that was scanned 'delivered' but that the recipient has not received. Email Investigations once per consignment. Include the tracking ID, the delivery scan time and location, and the recipient's statement of where they have checked. We reply with a PT- trace reference and a time for first findings, normally within two working days. Only one trace can be open for each consignment. A second request for the same consignment is merged into the open trace and restarts its findings clock. To chase a trace or add information, reply quoting its PT- reference. Trace findings are not a liability decision. Replacements and refunds remain the merchant's decision.
