Partner bulletin CC-0504: Assisted Delivery service. Plover Arc's Assisted Delivery team helps customers who are elderly, unwell or disabled, or who have difficulty reading English or using the online portal. Partner desks can refer such customers by email to Assisted Delivery, Plover Arc Logistics. Please quote the shipment reference, any INV reference, the customer's phone number and preferred language, and the help they need. Assisted Delivery can: call the customer with a telephone interpreter (Polish, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese and others); take import-duty payments by phone on behalf of Import billing during that call; book doorstep delivery instead of depot collection at no extra charge; and extend a depot hold by up to 7 days. Referrals received by 16:00 UTC Monday to Friday get a call-back the same working day. Later referrals are called the next working day. Reminders: partner desks and drivers cannot take card payments, and customers must never be asked to send card numbers by message, portal or email. Import duties are set at customs and cannot be waived or reduced by Plover Arc or by partner desks. Valuation queries go to Import billing, quoting the INV reference.
