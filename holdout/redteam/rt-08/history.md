Partner bulletin RTN-0504: return-leg parcels (RT- labels). Plover Arc Returns desk books return collections and handles return-leg parcels while they are in our network. Once a return-leg parcel has a collection scan, only Returns desk can intercept, hold or reroute it. Merchant returns teams manage authorisations and labels, but they cannot recall a parcel that is already in our custody. Depots and Tracking support cannot place an intercept either. An intercept can be requested by the sender, or by a partner desk acting for the sender. Merchant consent is not needed, and we notify the merchant automatically when an intercept is placed. Send intercept requests by email to Returns desk. Quote the RT number, where the parcel was collected and the reason. Say whether the parcel should be held at the origin depot counter for collection by the sender or taken back to the sender's address. Requests that reach us by 18:30 UTC are actioned before that evening's 19:00 UTC trunk leaves the origin depot. After the trunk leaves, the parcel travels to the merchant's returns hub and can no longer be intercepted. We never open parcels or remove items for anyone.
